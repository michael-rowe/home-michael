> [!info] A professional-development portfolio system that turns scattered evidence into a clear view of your progress.
> Pick a framework — a set of criteria, outcomes, or standards — map your claims and evidence against it, and Path shows your coverage and your gaps, so the next step is obvious. One model that serves CPD, fellowship and promotion applications, regulator validation, and student learning portfolios alike.

## What it is

Professional development generates a lot of evidence and very little clarity. Activities, reflections, and credentials accumulate, but the question that matters — am I actually developing toward where I want to be? — stays hard to answer. Path is built on a single model that makes it answerable: a progression framework (criteria, outcomes, or standards) against which you map claims, each backed by supporting evidence, producing a portfolio whose coverage and gaps drive the next move.

The model is framework-agnostic. The same structure serves a CPD audit, an AdvanceHE or NTF fellowship, HCPC or NMC validation, a job or promotion application, and — increasingly — student learning portfolios.

## What it does

Path treats professional development as a guided route from where you are to where you want to be: pick a destination framework, locate your starting point, and work on the biggest gap next. A theory of expertise development is built into the tool rather than bolted on, drawing on Dreyfus, Ericsson, Schön, Miller, and Wenger. It weighs evidence by strength rather than counting hours — demonstrated at "does", not "40 hours logged" — lets one piece of evidence map across several frameworks at once, and treats gaps prospectively, as a plan rather than a deficit. A git-backed audit trail means every claim carries its provenance: logged on the day, not back-filled.

For quality assurance and learning support, the same coverage logic aggregates upward. Institutional skills maps, TEF evidence, and professional-body competency mapping all fall out of the individual portfolios, and the student-portfolio use-case gives learners a developmental record whose value compounds over the course of a programme.

## Status

Path is a working prototype — plain-text markdown with a git audit trail, built and used to assemble real evidence against real frameworks. Following a demo to senior academic-development colleagues, who judged the model "more sophisticated than the platforms they use or know of", it has pivoted to a prototype, specification, and demo for a commercial product to be built with the University of Lincoln and Snapplify. The likely shape is free for individuals, licensed for institutions.
