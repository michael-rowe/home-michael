**Getting It Done Conference** · Lincolnshire Local Medical Committee · 18 September 2025

*Invited presentation*

## Overview

This presentation explores how generative AI can act as a virtual business consultant for GP practice managers — making sophisticated business development support accessible without specialist expertise or significant cost. The talk moves through six practical use cases: building a compelling online presence, targeting patient demographic groups, enhancing patient experience through education and content, strategic service development using deep research tools, ethical implementation and data protection policies, and managing the change process with staff. Each use case includes example prompts and AI-generated outputs demonstrated with real practice scenarios.
