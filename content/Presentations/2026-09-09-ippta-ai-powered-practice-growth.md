**IPPTA Business Meeting 2026** · International Private Physiotherapy Association · 9 September 2026

*Full-day workshop*

## Overview

A full-day, hands-on workshop for private practice owners at the [International Private Physiotherapy Association](https://ippta.org/)'s business meeting in Hamburg, run as four 90-minute sessions that each build on the one before it.

The premise is that practice owners already have the expertise; what they don't have is the time to turn it into content, communication, and a plan. So the day is built around production rather than explanation. Delegates work on their own practice throughout, keeping everything they produce in a single plain-text **practice file** that carries context between sessions — the answer to the context window, which is the one technical idea the day insists on.

The sessions:

- **Foundations** — how generative AI works, what the context window is and why it governs everything else, what to keep out of it, and the structure of a prompt (role, context, task, constraints, output) arrived at by building one by hand rather than by being taught it
- **Creating content** — naming real client groups, building one out as a persona with the model filling gaps rather than inventing the customer, drafting a newsletter for that one person, teaching the model your voice, and spinning the piece into five formats
- **Getting found and trusted** — reviewing a practice website as a prospective client in pain, an AI-use policy clients can read, a staff guide for the conversation that starts *"ChatGPT told me…"*, and a communication plan with an owner and a measure on every row
- **Growing the business** — a walkthrough of a completed Deep Research report, then delegates run their own (or take the interview route on tools without it), closing with the most important prompt of the day: *now argue against it*

Everything demonstrated runs on free tiers. The worked examples come from a real Lincolnshire practice website and a fictional practice plan, and the day closes with an AI-generated handover report so delegates leave with one page they can act on.
