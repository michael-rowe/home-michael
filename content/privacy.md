The site collects very little data about you. Here's what it does collect, and what happens to it.

## Analytics

This site uses [Umami](https://umami.is/), a privacy-focused analytics tool. Umami:

- Does not use cookies
- Does not collect personal information
- Does not track you across websites
- Only records anonymous, aggregated data (page views, referrers, device types)
- Is hosted in the EU and complies with GDPR

You cannot be identified from the analytics data collected.

## Newsletter

The newsletter is managed through [Kit](https://kit.com/) (formerly ConvertKit). When you subscribe:

- Your email address is stored by Kit
- You'll receive emails when I publish the newsletter (typically monthly)
- You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any email
- Your email is never shared with third parties
- Kit's privacy policy applies: [kit.com/privacy](https://kit.com/privacy)

## Contact form

The contact form uses [Web3Forms](https://web3forms.com/). When you submit a message:

- Your name, email address, message, and (on the speaking form) organisation are sent to me by email
- Web3Forms does not store your submission long-term
- I receive your message directly and may respond via email
- Your information is not shared with third parties

## Cookies

This site doesn't set any cookies. Your choice of light or dark mode, your graph settings and the graph's record of which pages you've visited are kept in your browser's local storage, which stays on your device and isn't sent anywhere.

## Third-party embeds

Some pages may include embedded content (videos, diagrams, forms). These embeds may set their own cookies according to their respective privacy policies.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** any personal data I hold about you
- **Delete** your data (unsubscribe from the newsletter)
- **Contact me** with any privacy concerns

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, [[contact|get in touch]].
