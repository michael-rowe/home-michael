**In Beta** · 29 April 2024

Danelle Hess describes what clinical placements ask of physiotherapy students in South Africa: caseloads managed with very little supervision, cases including gunshot wounds and poly-trauma, and coping strategies built for survival rather than for learning to reason well. We talk about the mental health cost of that, and what universities can put around students who are carrying it.
