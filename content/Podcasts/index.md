## In Beta

The podcast I co-founded with Ben Ellis — conversations with physiotherapy and health professions educators about teaching, curriculum and, latterly, what AI was going to do to both. Thirty-eight episodes between 2017 and 2024.

The show lives at [inbetaphysio.com](https://inbetaphysio.com/category/podcasts/) and that is where you listen to it. Each episode has a page here with the date, running time and a short summary, and every one of them sends you to the original post for the recording and the full show notes.

## SAAHE

Between 2017 and 2019 I ran a podcast for the [South African Association of Health Educationists](https://saahe.org.za/), mostly interviews with people who had just finished a PhD in health professions education and had something worth hearing about how they did it. Eight episodes.

The recordings are no longer online. SAAHE's site kept the show notes through a rebuild and dropped the audio, so these pages are what remains of the series in public. I still have the files; where they end up is an open question.
