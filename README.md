# /home/michael

> Practical thinking about AI in health professions education

This site is for **health professions educators** navigating the integration of AI into teaching, learning, clinical supervision, and academic practice — medical educators, clinical supervisors, curriculum designers, faculty developers, and anyone involved in developing health professionals.

The work here takes AI seriously as a professional challenge, not just a technical one. The questions are practical: How should we redesign assessment when AI can complete most of what we currently ask students to do? What does AI literacy mean for a physiotherapist or a clinical supervisor? How do we develop our own AI capabilities without losing what makes education distinctively human?

## What's here

- **Essays** — long-form academic writing on AI, pedagogy, and health professions education, ranging from early drafts to peer-reviewed publications
- **Courses** — structured learning sequences on AI literacy, educational technology, and professional practice
- **Notes** — shorter concept pieces connecting ideas across the literature
- **Presentations** — slides and context from conference and invited talks
- **Newsletter** — a monthly digest of ideas, readings, and work in progress

Browse the [live site](https://michael-rowe.github.io/home-michael/) to explore content organised by what you're trying to learn or do.

## About the author

[Michael Rowe](https://michael-rowe.github.io/home-michael/) is Director of Teaching and Learning at the University of Lincoln, UK. His background is in physiotherapy education, and his current work focuses on AI integration, digital transformation, and what it means to practise scholarship differently in a period of rapid change.

## How it's built

The site is built with [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/) and deployed via GitHub Pages.

```
Obsidian (Markdown) → Git/GitHub → Quartz → GitHub Pages
                                      ↓
                             OSF (DOI + Preprint)
```

Essays follow a semantic versioning system reflecting their stage of development:

| Version | Stage |
|---------|-------|
| 0.1–0.6 | Working draft |
| 0.7–0.8 | Preprint deposited with DOI |
| 0.9 | Submitted to peer review |
| 1.0+ | Published in a peer-reviewed venue |
